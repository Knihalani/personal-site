Welcome to my corner of the sky. Every island has a signpost: walk up to one and read it. Eden lies north across the long bridge, J to the east, Contact to the south, and About to the west over the stepping stones.

Puffles follow anyone holding a flower, kits love sticks, and the sky jellies drift after lanterns. Right click to pet them. Mine blocks, craft in your satchel, build whatever you like: the signs stay put.
