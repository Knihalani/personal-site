Welcome · the Hub

Kaiav Nihalani

Founder of Eden. Co-founder of J.

Welcome to my corner of the sky. Every island has a waystone sign: read it to light it up, then travel between them from your journal. Eden is north, J is east, Contact is south and About is west.

Grab the sky sail from the stand by the plaza and ride the updrafts between islands. Glowing shards hide all over the archipelago. Feed a creature its favourite treat and it will join you. Falling is fine: a sky jelly always catches you.
