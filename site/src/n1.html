Welcome · Home Isle

Kaiav Nihalani

Founder of Eden. Co-founder of J.

Welcome to my corner of the sky. At the heart of the island stands the Memory Engine: walk up to its console and press E to watch the whole story. You can replay it any time from your journal.

Grab the sky sail from the stand by the plaza and ride the updrafts out to the little islets. Sky relics hide out there and stitch upgrades into your sail. Beyond the banner buoys lie the Wilds: ruins, monsters and loot. Feed a creature its favourite treat and it will join you. Falling is fine: a sky jelly always catches you.
